Privacy Policy

ComboCrush: Combo Blast (package com.combocrush.game)
Effective date: November 25, 2026 · Applies to version 1.4.0 and later

This policy explains what information ComboCrush: Combo Blast ("the app", "we") handles. The short version: we do not collect, store, or sell any personal information ourselves. The app uses two third-party services from Google LLC: Google AdMob (for ads) and, optionally on Android, Google Play Games Services (for global leaderboards, achievements, and cloud saves).

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no ComboCrush-specific accounts, passwords, or sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We run no servers of our own that receive your data.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress, stats, settings (sound, music, haptics, themes), unlocked themes and specials, token balance, daily reward streak, puzzle progress, local achievements, and local leaderboards ("My Best", including any arcade initials or nickname you type for a high score) are stored locally on your device. This data remains on your device unless you choose to sign in to Google Play Games Services on Android for Cloud Saves, and local data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

3. Google Play Games Services (Optional, Android)

On Android, the app integrates Google Play Games Services v2, a service of Google LLC, to provide optional Global Leaderboards, Achievements (Badges), and Cloud Saves. Signing in is optional—you can play the game offline with local leaderboards and local badges without signing in.

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google may process:

  • Play Games Profile & Player ID: your Play Games gamer tag (display name) and player identifier, used to display your sign-in status, show your rank on Global Leaderboards, and sync achievements.
  • Global Leaderboards & Achievements: your personal best scores per game mode and unlocked achievement badges are submitted to Google Play Games Services. Whether your scores appear publicly on Global Leaderboards is controlled by your Play Games privacy settings.
  • Cloud Saves (Google Drive AppData): game progress (stats, personal best scores, puzzle progress, unlocked themes, specials vault, token balance, daily reward streak, arcade initials, and unlocked achievements) is backed up in your private Google Play Games / Google Drive AppData storage managed by Google so progress can restore across reinstalls and Android devices. We have no direct server access to your Google account or Drive files.

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Google processes this data under its own policies and controls:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Play Games profile privacy & other settings
  • Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate (not precise) location
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (ad views and taps) and crash or diagnostic information related to ads

Google processes this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob data disclosure

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS).

5. Device permissions

  • Internet: used to load ads (Google AdMob) and, when signed in on Android, to sync Cloud Saves, Global Leaderboards, and Achievements with Google Play Games Services.
  • Vibrate: used for haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data deletion

  • Local device data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Google Play Games & Cloud Save data: delete ComboCrush game data or your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app (Profile → Settings → Privacy & settings → Delete profile & game data) or see Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile.
  • Advertising data: reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings, or see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/gcerkez/combocrush-web/issues or use the developer email address listed on the app's Google Play page.
